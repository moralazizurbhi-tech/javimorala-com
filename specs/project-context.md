# Project Context: Javi Morala

## Vision

Javi Morala has an authentic, self-owned personal space that presents himself
as a person to the world — carrying a distinctly modern, alternative, and
experimental character, echoing the same experimental design sensibility the
site itself embodies. The site exists primarily as self-expression: success
means Javi's identity and vision come through genuinely to whoever
encounters it. The design capability, implementation ability, and effective
AI-assisted development the site demonstrates are a natural consequence of
that self-expression, not a separate goal pursued for its own sake.

## Problem Space

- No personal web presence exists for Javi Morala today — not at
  javimorala.com, not anywhere else. Greenfield: nothing to build on,
  migrate from, or reconcile with.

## Stakeholders

- **Javi Morala** — sole creator and maintainer, and the primary beneficiary.
  This is built for himself, as self-expression, not commissioned or aimed
  at serving any particular external party's purpose.
- **Site visitors** — a general, unqualified audience: anyone who chooses to
  enter the site. Not curated toward recruiters, clients, collaborators, or
  any specific professional segment. Their relevance is simply that they are
  the ones who may "feel" Javi's vision when they arrive.

## Constraints

- Solo effort — only Javi Morala builds and maintains the site; no
  collaborators or organizational support to draw on.
- No fixed deadline — self-paced, proceeding at his own pace rather than
  externally timed.

## Context

- Purely personal initiative: no employer, organization, or third-party
  involvement or oversight.
- javimorala.com is the intended domain for the site.
