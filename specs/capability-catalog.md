# Capability Catalog: Javi Morala

## Capabilities

- **id:** `hero-presentation`
  **name:** Hero Presentation
  **definition:** Deliver an immediate, high-impact first impression of Javi's
  identity and character to any visitor arriving at the site.

- **id:** `about-narrative`
  **name:** About Narrative
  **definition:** Present Javi's identity authentically, in his own voice, as
  self-expression rather than persuasion toward any particular audience.

- **id:** `direct-contact`
  **name:** Direct Contact
  **definition:** Let an interested visitor reach Javi directly through an
  external channel (e.g. email), without an on-site form or data
  collection.

- **id:** `presence-links`
  **name:** Presence Links
  **definition:** Let a visitor discover Javi's broader online presence
  through links to his external profiles, distinct from direct contact.

- **id:** `localization`
  **name:** Localization
  **definition:** Present all site content in the visitor's language
  (English, Castellano, or Euskera), auto-detected from browser locale by
  default, with an always-available manual override that is remembered
  for future visits.

- **id:** `section-navigation`
  **name:** Section Navigation
  **definition:** Let a visitor move directly between screens via a
  persistent navigation aid — inline on desktop, overlay on mobile — as
  a shortcut alongside free scrolling.

## Excluded

- **Motion & Interaction** — not a capability. It has no independent,
  visitor-initiated outcome; it describes *how* every capability above is
  presented. Already captured as a Functional Principle in Project Design
  and in Project UX's Visual Foundations/Constraints; not duplicated here.
- **Accessibility** — not a capability. A cross-cutting quality constraint
  applying to every capability above, already captured in Project Design
  and Project UX; not duplicated here.
- **Visual Foundations** (typography, colour, spacing) — not a capability.
  Shared visual language, not a functional outcome.
- **Scroll/Continue Cue** — not a standalone capability. A motion-expressed
  detail supporting free scrolling, with no independent outcome beyond
  what Section Navigation and free scrolling already provide.
- **AI-Assisted Development Note** — Let a visitor learn, through a small and low-key mention,
  that the site itself was built through AI-assisted development. This should
  be inside the about narrative, as part of the content
