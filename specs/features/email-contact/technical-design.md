# Technical Design: Email Contact

## Technical Components

### Email Contact Device

Proposed new component, composed within Project Architecture's Contact
Section (Existing), elaborating only this Feature's email-owned slice;
the social-links-owned element within Contact Section is out of scope,
left to `social-links`' own future Technical Design.

**Purpose**

Realize `email-contact`'s Feature Contract by presenting the single
primary email-initiation trigger, doubling as the Contact view's sign-off
message, with non-scrapable address exposure and a no-client fallback.

**Responsibilities**

- Present the single primary trigger element that both carries the
  sign-off/closing message text and initiates a client-side email action
  addressed to Javi's one destination address.
- Resolve/expose the destination address through a mechanism that avoids
  literal plain-text exposure in rendered content/source.
- Provide a fallback means of obtaining the destination address when the
  primary trigger doesn't result in a mail client opening, itself also
  non-plain-text-exposed.
- Keep the trigger element and sign-off message a single inseparable
  presentational unit.

**Owned Concepts**

- *Email-initiation trigger* — the single activatable element that both
  displays the sign-off message and initiates the client-side email
  action. Owned exclusively here.
- *Address exposure guard* — the requirement that the destination address
  never appears as directly-scrapable plain text, applying to both the
  primary trigger and fallback. Owned exclusively here (the concrete
  technique is Implementation, not decided here).
- *No-client fallback path* — the alternate means, provided by this
  component, for obtaining the destination address when the primary
  trigger's client-side action doesn't open a mail client. Owned
  exclusively here.

**Collaborations**

- Consumes locale-resolved sign-off/CTA text from the Locale Layer
  (Existing pattern, same as Hero Section).
- Composed within Contact Section alongside the social-links-owned
  element (not designed here).
- Uses the Animation Layer for any interaction/motion presentation;
  motion behavior itself is `motion-interaction`'s decision, out of
  boundary.
- Consumes the Styling System's shared visual tokens/mixins; concrete
  visual/interaction treatment deferred to Feature UX.

**Dependencies**

- Locale Layer (content resolution).
- Animation Layer (interaction presentation mechanics).
- Styling System (visual tokens).

**Constraints**

- Must not depend on or invoke `section-navigation`'s or `social-links`'
  components — excluded from this Feature's boundary.
- Must not implement or request any in-app form submission or
  backend/API call for the email action — client-side email-initiation
  only (per Project Architecture: Contact Section connects outbound only
  via native browser links).
- Must not expose the destination address as literal plain text in
  rendered/source content, in either the primary or fallback
  presentation.
- Must not decide the concrete address-exposure-guard technique, fallback
  presentation mechanism, or visual/interaction treatment — the technique
  is Implementation; the visual/interaction treatment is Feature UX.

**Design Decisions**

- Modeled as a single component, not split into separate
  trigger/fallback/address-source components — the Contract requires
  them to function as one inseparable, consistent unit (Commitment 4;
  Commitment 1 AC3); splitting risks divergence rather than reflecting a
  natural technical boundary.
- Address Exposure Guard is modeled as an owned requirement/concept, not
  a concrete mechanism — avoids committing to a technique at this
  implementation-independent phase.
- The no-client fallback is modeled as an alternate presentational path
  of this same component, not a separate component — mirrors Feature
  Solution's framing of it as an alternative outcome of the same
  activation, not a distinct state.
- The destination address is treated as a single, locale-invariant piece
  of data supplied to (not generated by) this component — reflects
  Commitment 1 AC3's cross-locale/session consistency requirement,
  without deciding the concrete data-sourcing mechanism.

**Contract Traceability**

- Commitment 1 (Primary Email-Initiation Trigger) — Responsibility 1,
  Owned Concept "Email-initiation trigger," Design Decision 4, no-form/
  backend Constraint.
- Commitment 2 (Non-Plain-Text Address Exposure) — Responsibility 2,
  Owned Concept "Address exposure guard," Design Decision 2, exposure
  Constraint.
- Commitment 3 (No-Client Fallback Availability) — Responsibility 3,
  Owned Concept "No-client fallback path," Design Decision 3.
- Commitment 4 (Sign-Off/Trigger Inseparability) — Responsibilities 1 &
  4, Owned Concept "Email-initiation trigger," Design Decision 1.

## Cross-Component Relationships

- Contact Section → Email Contact Device: composes it (Existing parent,
  partially elaborated here; the remaining social-links-owned portion is
  out of scope).
- Email Contact Device → Locale Layer: consumes resolved text (Existing
  pattern).
- Email Contact Device → Animation Layer: supplies elements for
  interaction presentation; no state/completeness concept exposed.
- Email Contact Device → Styling System: consumes shared visual tokens
  (Existing pattern).
- Email Contact Device ⇸ `section-navigation`, `social-links`: explicitly
  no dependency (boundary preserved).

No circular dependencies: Email Contact Device depends outward on Locale
Layer, Animation Layer, and Styling System; Contact Section composes it
inbound only; none depend back.
