# Feature Context: About Narrative

**Status:** Approved

## Problem

After the Hero's first impression, a visitor has no deeper sense of who
Javi actually is — his authentic personal/professional identity and
background — nor any awareness that the site itself was built through
AI-assisted development. Without resolving this, the visitor's engagement
stops at a surface-level impression and the site's self-expression vision
goes unrealized once they scroll past the Hero.

## Motivation

Directly serves Project Context's Vision: "success means Javi's identity
and vision come through genuinely to whoever encounters it." This is the
phase of the experience where that identity is delivered in full depth, in
Javi's own voice — plus honestly and quietly naming that AI-assisted
development played a role in building the site itself, per Project
Definition's "effective use of AI-assisted development" thread and Project
Design's "show, don't just tell" principle.

## Scope

### Included

- Conveying Javi's authentic personal/professional identity, in depth,
  beyond the Hero's first impression.
- Visually grounding that identity with one or two personal photos.
- Surfacing, briefly and without overshadowing, that the site was built
  through AI-assisted development.
- Holding all of the above together as one coherent problem/moment, not
  fragmented content blocks.

### Excluded

- The visitor's first-impression/arrival moment — `hero-presentation`'s
  problem.
- Wayfinding to reach this screen — `section-navigation`'s problem.
- Enabling actual outreach/contact — `direct-contact` / `presence-links`'s
  problem.
- Resolving content into the visitor's language —
  `content-localization`'s problem.
- The site's motion/interaction feel in general — `motion-interaction`'s
  problem.
- Demonstrating Javi's work through a project catalog/portfolio —
  excluded project-wide (Design Constraints).

## Constraints

- Must express within the established visual identity (palette,
  experimental character) — inherited, same as Hero Presentation.
- Content must read as authentic self-expression in Javi's own voice, not
  persuasion-oriented copy.
- The AI-assisted-development note must stay small/low-key — "quiet
  enough that it never competes with the narrative for attention."
- No work/portfolio-showcase content.
- No final photo assets exist yet; placeholders stand in until Javi
  selects them.
- Inherited from project level: solo effort, no fixed deadline,
  static-generated architecture with no backend.

## Known Dependencies

- **`content-localization`** — narrative and note text depend on this
  Feature being resolved in the right language.
- **`section-navigation`** — enables reaching this screen; not part of
  this Feature's own composition.
- **`motion-interaction`** — governs any reveal/motion applied to this
  Feature's elements.
- **`accessibility`** — cross-cutting; applies to text and photo
  elements.
