# Project Design: Javi Morala

## Solution Overview

Javi Morala's site presents his identity to the world through a single, continuously
scrolling page, organized into three functional domains — Introduction, Personal
Narrative, and Connection — that first make an impression, then describe who Javi is,
and finally offer a direct way to reach him. The site is presented in English and
Spanish to reach as wide a personal and professional audience as possible. The site's
own execution — a coherent, modern design carried consistently across every section —
serves as direct, tangible
evidence of Javi's technical and design capability, without a separate portfolio or
work-showcase capability. Contact happens entirely through external channels, keeping
the product free of backend complexity and consistent with a solo, statically-authored
effort.

## Functional Domains

- **Introduction** — give any visitor an immediate, high-impact first impression of who
  Javi Morala is.
- **Personal Narrative** — present Javi's professional and personal identity
  authentically, to build credibility and connection.
- **Connection** — enable an interested visitor to reach Javi through external channels.

## Product Capabilities

- **Hero presentation** (Introduction domain) — immediate visual/text impression of
  Javi's identity and value.
- **About narrative** (Personal Narrative domain) — authentic bio content spanning
  professional and personal identity.
- **Contact links** (Connection domain) — direct links to external contact channels
  (email/social).

## Functional Principles

- **Show, don't just tell** — the site's own craftsmanship, across every domain, is
  itself the primary evidence of Javi's capability, not stated claims.
- **Authenticity** — content in every domain reflects Javi's genuine voice rather than
  generic or templated language.

## Cross-Functional Rules

- All three domains present as sections of one continuous single-page experience, not
  separately navigated pages.
- Visual and design coherence is consistent across all domains, since "show, don't tell"
  depends on the whole experience reading as one deliberate, unified piece of work.
- No domain requires backend processing, data persistence, or user accounts; contact
  resolves through external channels only.
- All domain content (Introduction, Personal Narrative, and Connection) is available in
  both English and Spanish; the visitor's language is auto-detected from browser locale
  by default, with a manual override always available.

## Design Constraints

- No work/portfolio-showcase capability — demonstration relies on the site's own
  execution, not a listed project catalog.
- Contact resolves via external links only (email/social) — no on-site form or visitor
  data collection.
- Content is authored directly and solely by Javi Morala; no CMS or multi-author
  workflow implied.
- Bilingual content (English/Spanish) is authored directly by Javi Morala for each
  domain; no translation service is used.
