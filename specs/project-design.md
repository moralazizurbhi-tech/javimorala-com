# Project Design: Javi Morala

## Solution Overview

Javi Morala's site presents his identity to the world through a single,
continuously scrolling page, working across mobile and desktop devices and
accessible to visitors regardless of ability. A deliberately modern,
alternative, experimental character is the defining creative thread of the
whole experience — not a decorative layer, but the core statement, carried
consistently through Introduction, Personal Narrative, and Connection: the
three functional domains that first make a high-impact impression, then
express who Javi is in his own voice as self-expression (including a
small, low-key note on the AI-assisted development behind the site), and
finally let an interested visitor both reach him directly and discover more
about him through his broader online presence — with that same
experimental character held to the very end. The site's own execution,
where motion and interaction are a deliberate part of that character rather
than incidental polish, is the primary evidence of Javi's design,
implementation, and AI-assisted-development capability, without a separate
portfolio or work-showcase feature. Content is offered in English, Spanish,
and Euskera at launch, with the underlying approach built to extend to
further languages over time, and the single page provides a way to orient
and move between its domains without introducing separate navigated pages.
Contact and presence links resolve entirely through external channels,
keeping the product free of backend complexity and consistent with a solo,
statically-authored effort.

## Functional Domains

- **Introduction** — give any visitor an immediate, high-impact first
  impression of who Javi Morala is, leading with the modern, alternative,
  experimental character that defines the whole experience.
- **Personal Narrative** — present Javi's identity authentically, in his
  own voice and through that same modern, experimental sensibility, as
  self-expression rather than persuasion toward any particular external
  outcome or audience segment; also carries the small note on the site's
  AI-assisted development process.
- **Connection** — enable an interested visitor both to reach Javi directly
  and to discover more about him through his broader online presence,
  keeping the same experimental character consistent to the end of the
  experience.

## Product Capabilities

- **Hero presentation** (Introduction domain) — immediate visual/text
  impression of Javi's identity and character.
- **About narrative** (Personal Narrative domain) — authentic personal and
  professional identity content, in Javi's own voice.
- **AI-assisted development note** (Personal Narrative domain) — a small,
  low-key mention naming that the site was built through AI-assisted
  development, without making it a headline feature.
- **Direct contact** (Connection domain) — a direct way to reach Javi (e.g.
  email).
- **Presence links** (Connection domain) — links to Javi's broader online
  presence (social/professional profiles), distinct from direct contact.

## Functional Principles

- **Modern & experimental character** — a deliberately modern, alternative,
  experimental feel is the defining thread running through every domain
  and capability; it is the core creative statement of the site, expressed
  consistently in visuals, motion, interaction, and voice, not a
  decorative add-on.
- **Show, don't just tell** — the site's own craftsmanship, across every
  domain, is itself the primary evidence of Javi's design, implementation,
  and AI-assisted-development capability, not stated claims.
- **Authenticity** — content in every domain reflects Javi's genuine voice,
  as self-expression rather than persuasion aimed at any particular
  visitor segment.
- **Motion & interaction as expression** — how the site moves and responds
  to interaction is a deliberate, first-class part of demonstrating the
  experimental design character, not incidental polish.

## Cross-Functional Rules

- All three domains present as sections of one continuous single-page
  experience, not separately navigated pages.
- The single page provides a way for visitors to orient and move between
  domains (e.g., a quick way to reach Connection from anywhere), without
  introducing a separate multi-page navigation system.
- Visual and design coherence is consistent across all domains, since
  "show, don't tell" depends on the whole experience reading as one
  deliberate, unified piece of work.
- No domain requires backend processing, data persistence, or user
  accounts; contact and presence links resolve through external channels
  only.
- All domain content is available in English, Spanish, and Euskera at
  launch; the approach is designed to extend to additional languages over
  time. Visitor language is auto-detected from browser locale by default,
  with a manual override always available.

## Design Constraints

- No work/portfolio-showcase capability — demonstration relies on the
  site's own execution, not a listed project catalog.
- Contact and presence links resolve via external channels only — no
  on-site form or visitor data collection.
- Content across all domains and languages is authored directly and solely
  by Javi Morala — no CMS or multi-author workflow implied.
- The experience must work across mobile and desktop devices, and must
  remain accessible to visitors regardless of ability (e.g.,
  assistive-technology compatibility, sufficient visual contrast, full
  keyboard operability). The specific accessibility standard or
  conformance level is left to a later phase.
